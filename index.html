What it does

Point

An open hand moves the cursor.

Click

Close your fist, or tap thumb and index together.

Drag

Keep the fist closed and move.

Scroll

At the speed you set, in the direction you like.

Zoom

Frame the screen with both hands.

Switch desktops

A sideways swipe moves you to the next one.

Volume

Hold your hand half closed, as if around a ball, and turn it: clockwise is louder.

Dictation

Speak instead of typing, when you switch it on.

Free

SkyHand is free. Custom gestures, your own shortcuts on your own moves, are coming soon. It runs on your computer; the camera image never leaves it.
